How to Join FMA

Complete the steps below to join FMA for the semester.

1

STEP ONE

Join FMA on MineTracker

Join the official Financial Management Association organization on MineTracker to connect with the chapter and receive organization updates.

Join on MineTracker ↗
2

STEP TWO

Pay the Semester Membership Fee

SEMESTER MEMBERSHIP

$25

per member · per semester

Membership dues may be paid at any point during the semester using either Cash App or Zelle.

Both payment methods are accepted equally.

REQUIRED PAYMENT MEMO

Full Name – FMA Membership

Please enter this format in the payment note so the Treasurer can match your payment to your membership.

PAYMENT METHOD

Cash App

Scan the QR code using Cash App and send the $25 semester membership fee.

Memo: Full Name – FMA Membership

PAYMENT METHOD

Zelle

Scan the QR code using your banking app or Zelle and send the $25 semester membership fee.

Memo: Full Name – FMA Membership

Please note: Membership dues are non-refundable.
3

STEP THREE

You're Done

After completing your payment, no additional payment form is required. An FMA officer will verify your membership.
